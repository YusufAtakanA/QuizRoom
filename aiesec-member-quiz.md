# AIESEC Member Quiz

Two QuizRoom quizzes of 10 questions each (a quiz holds at most 10 approved questions). The correct answer is in **bold**.

---

## Quiz 1: AIESEC Basics

**1. In which year was AIESEC founded?**
- A) 1938
- **B) 1948**
- C) 1958
- D) 1968

*Explanation:* AIESEC was founded in 1948, after the Second World War, by students from several European countries.

**2. What is AIESEC's vision?**
- A) Education for every young person
- **B) Peace and fulfillment of humankind's potential**
- C) A world without borders
- D) Leadership for a sustainable economy

*Explanation:* AIESEC's vision is "Peace and fulfillment of humankind's potential".

**3. What did the name AIESEC originally stand for?**
- A) Association for International Exchange of Students in Economics and Culture
- **B) Association Internationale des Étudiants en Sciences Économiques et Commerciales**
- C) Alliance of International Entrepreneurs and Student Exchange Clubs
- D) Association of International Educators for Social and Economic Change

*Explanation:* It was a French acronym. Today the organisation simply uses the name AIESEC.

**4. Who runs AIESEC?**
- A) University professors
- B) A board of corporate partners
- **C) Young people**
- D) National governments

*Explanation:* AIESEC is a non-governmental, not-for-profit organisation run entirely by young people.

**5. What is AIESEC's political stance?**
- A) It supports a political party in each country
- **B) It is non-political**
- C) It is part of the United Nations
- D) It is run by the EU

*Explanation:* AIESEC is independent and non-political.

**6. Which of these is NOT one of AIESEC's exchange programs?**
- A) Global Volunteer
- B) Global Talent
- C) Global Teacher
- **D) Global Student**

*Explanation:* AIESEC's exchange programs are Global Volunteer, Global Talent and Global Teacher.

**7. What does a Global Volunteer participant usually do?**
- A) A paid long-term internship at a company
- **B) A short-term volunteer project linked to the Sustainable Development Goals**
- C) A semester of university study abroad
- D) A teaching degree

*Explanation:* Global Volunteer projects are short-term social impact projects connected to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

**8. What does Global Talent offer?**
- A) Volunteering in schools
- **B) Professional internships abroad**
- C) Online courses
- D) Sports exchanges

*Explanation:* Global Talent offers professional internships with organisations abroad.

**9. Which UN agenda are AIESEC's volunteer projects aligned with?**
- A) The Millennium Development Goals
- **B) The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**
- C) The Kyoto Protocol
- D) The Bretton Woods agreements

*Explanation:* AIESEC projects contribute to the 17 Sustainable Development Goals.

**10. In roughly how many countries and territories is AIESEC present?**
- A) About 20
- B) About 50
- **C) Over 100**
- D) Over 300

*Explanation:* AIESEC is active in over 100 countries and territories.

## Quiz 2: Leadership, Values and Structure

**1. How many qualities are in AIESEC's leadership model?**
- A) 3
- **B) 4**
- C) 5
- D) 6

*Explanation:* The model has four qualities: Self-aware, Solution-oriented, World citizen and Empowering others.

**2. Which of these is a quality in AIESEC's leadership model?**
- A) Profit-driven
- **B) World citizen**
- C) Competitive
- D) Independent thinker

*Explanation:* World citizen is one of the four leadership qualities.

**3. Which of these is NOT an AIESEC leadership quality?**
- A) Self-aware
- B) Solution-oriented
- C) Empowering others
- **D) Risk-taking**

*Explanation:* The four qualities are Self-aware, Solution-oriented, World citizen and Empowering others.

**4. Which of these is one of AIESEC's values?**
- **A) Living Diversity**
- B) Maximising Profit
- C) Following Orders
- D) Avoiding Risk

*Explanation:* AIESEC's values: Activating Leadership, Demonstrating Integrity, Enjoying Participation, Living Diversity, Acting Sustainably, Striving for Excellence.

**5. How many core values does AIESEC have?**
- A) 4
- B) 5
- **C) 6**
- D) 8

*Explanation:* There are six: Activating Leadership, Demonstrating Integrity, Enjoying Participation, Living Diversity, Acting Sustainably, Striving for Excellence.

**6. Which value is about being honest and transparent?**
- A) Acting Sustainably
- **B) Demonstrating Integrity**
- C) Enjoying Participation
- D) Striving for Excellence

*Explanation:* Demonstrating Integrity means being consistent, honest and transparent.

**7. What is the local level of AIESEC, usually based at a university or city, called?**
- **A) Local Committee (LC)**
- B) Member Committee (MC)
- C) Regional Office
- D) Student Chapter

*Explanation:* Local Committees run AIESEC's activities in a city or university.

**8. What is the national-level body of AIESEC in a country called?**
- A) Local Committee
- **B) Member Committee (MC)**
- C) National Board of Directors
- D) Country Council

*Explanation:* The Member Committee leads AIESEC at the national level.

**9. What is the global leadership body of AIESEC called?**
- A) AIESEC Global Board
- **B) AIESEC International (AI)**
- C) World Committee
- D) International Secretariat

*Explanation:* AIESEC International coordinates the organisation globally.

**10. In AIESEC language, what is an "EP"?**
- A) Executive President
- **B) Exchange Participant**
- C) External Partner
- D) Event Planner

*Explanation:* An Exchange Participant is someone who goes on an AIESEC exchange.

> AIESEC is a global, non-governmental, not-for-profit, non-political organisation run entirely by young people. It was founded in 1948, after the Second World War, by students from several European countries. The name was originally a French acronym, Association Internationale des Étudiants en Sciences Économiques et Commerciales. AIESEC's vision is "Peace and fulfillment of humankind's potential". It is active in over 100 countries and territories. AIESEC develops leadership in young people through cross-cultural exchanges and volunteering. Its exchange programs are Global Volunteer (short-term volunteer projects linked to the UN Sustainable Development Goals), Global Talent (professional internships abroad) and Global Teacher (teaching positions abroad). A person who goes on an exchange is called an Exchange Participant (EP). AIESEC's leadership model has four qualities: Self-aware, Solution-oriented, World citizen and Empowering others. AIESEC has six values: Activating Leadership, Demonstrating Integrity, Enjoying Participation, Living Diversity, Acting Sustainably and Striving for Excellence. AIESEC is organised in three levels: Local Committees (LC) in cities or universities, Member Committees (MC) at the national level, and AIESEC International (AI) at the global level.
